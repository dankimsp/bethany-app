About Bethany

Since 1982, Bethany Church has welcomed members of the greater Seattle community to join in song, service, worship and prayer. We are a multi-cultural, multi-ethnic church with English and Korean ministries.

Our mission: To educate the next generation to grow in the love of Christ, to be God's witnesses to "the ends of the earth," and to be the salt and light of the earth, serving others in love and sharing the Gospel with our community.
